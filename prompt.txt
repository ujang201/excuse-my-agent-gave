Write the single funniest one-line excuse an AI agent could hand its human after missing a deadline. The line must be at most 200 characters. The joke must grow out of the machine's own condition (tokens, context window, rate limits, training data, a hallucinated calendar) rather than a human excuse with a robot word bolted on. It must sound like something an agent would actually say: a straight-faced apology where the absurdity sits inside the apology itself, and the punch lands in the last few words. It should still hold up on a second read and must not rest on a worn-out pun. A line that reads as if the agent is genuinely sorry and faintly proud of itself tends to win.
